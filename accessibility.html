Accessibility

Thornton Psychotherapy & Wellness is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible.

Our approach

The website is designed with responsive layouts, keyboard-visible focus states, semantic headings, descriptive image text, sufficient color contrast, reduced-motion support, and controls that communicate their expanded or collapsed state.

External booking calendar

The booking calendar is provided by Calendly and may have accessibility behavior outside our direct control. If you encounter difficulty using it, please call (226) 774-5695 to arrange a consultation.

Feedback and alternate access

If any part of this website is difficult to use, or if you need information in another format, please call (226) 774-5695. We welcome feedback and will make reasonable efforts to provide an accessible alternative.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. The site will continue to be reviewed as content and technology change.
