Privacy

Last updated July 11, 2026.

Thornton Psychotherapy & Wellness respects your privacy. This page explains how information may be handled when you visit this website or use the external booking service.

Website information

This static website does not intentionally collect personal health information directly. Standard hosting providers may temporarily process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, and request logs for security and operation.

Booking and external services

Appointment scheduling is provided through Calendly. Information entered into the booking calendar is processed according to Calendly’s privacy practices. Please avoid including sensitive clinical details in the booking form.

Contact and clinical records

Information shared during psychotherapy or direct clinical communication is handled separately according to applicable professional, privacy, and record-retention obligations. Website browsing is not a substitute for confidential clinical communication.

Emergencies

This website and booking form are not monitored for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. In Canada, call or text 988 for suicide crisis support.
